# Quest Story: Shortened links for shared URLs

## Quest Title

Shortened links for shared URLs

## Problem Statement

Long product URLs are unwieldy to share in chat and print materials.

## Who Is Affected / Actors

Marketing staff who share product links.

## Current Behaviour

Marketing pastes the full, long URL everywhere it's shared.

## Use Cases

1. Marketing generates a short link for a product URL and shares it.
2. A visitor opens a short link and lands on the original product page.

## Desired Outcomes

Marketing can generate and share a short link that redirects to the original URL.

## Scope Boundaries

**In-Scope**: generating a short link for any existing URL; redirecting a short link to its target.
**Out-of-Scope**: custom vanity slugs; click analytics.
**Adjacent Concerns**: not requested — a separate future ask if it comes up.

## Success Criteria

A generated short link redirects to the correct original URL every time it's used.

## Assumptions & Known Unknowns

**Assumed**: nothing beyond what's stated above.
**Unknown**: nothing material identified.
