# Quest Story: Self-service password reset

## Quest Title

Self-service password reset

## Problem Statement

Customers who forget their password must contact support to regain access.

## Who Is Affected / Actors

Customers who forget their password.

## Current Behaviour

Support manually resets a password after a customer contacts them.

## Use Cases

1. Customer requests a reset link by email and sets a new password via the link.

## Desired Outcomes

Customers can reset a forgotten password themselves, without contacting support.

## Scope Boundaries

**In-Scope**: email-based reset-link flow.
**Out-of-Scope**: SMS-based reset.
**Adjacent Concerns**: not requested.

## Success Criteria

A customer can regain account access via a reset link without any support interaction.

## Assumptions & Known Unknowns

**Assumed**: nothing beyond what's stated above.
**Unknown**: nothing material identified.
