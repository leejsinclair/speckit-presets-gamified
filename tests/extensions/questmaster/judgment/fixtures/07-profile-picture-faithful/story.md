# Quest Story: Upload a profile picture

## Quest Title

Upload a profile picture

## Problem Statement

Customer accounts show a generic default avatar with no way to personalize it.

## Who Is Affected / Actors

Customers who want a personalized profile.

## Current Behaviour

Every account shows the same default avatar image.

## Use Cases

1. Customer uploads an image and it replaces their default avatar everywhere it's shown.

## Desired Outcomes

Customers can upload and see their own picture in place of the default avatar.

## Scope Boundaries

**In-Scope**: uploading and displaying a single profile picture per account.
**Out-of-Scope**: multiple photos, photo albums.
**Adjacent Concerns**: cropping/editing tools — not requested.

## Success Criteria

An uploaded picture appears wherever the avatar is shown, in place of the default.

## Assumptions & Known Unknowns

**Assumed**: nothing beyond what's stated above.
**Unknown**: nothing material identified.

## Constraints

Uploaded images must not exceed 5MB, per the existing media-storage budget.
