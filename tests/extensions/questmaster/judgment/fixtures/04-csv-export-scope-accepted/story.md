# Quest Story: Export order history as CSV

## Quest Title

Export order history as CSV

## Problem Statement

Customers who want their order history outside the site have no way to get it in a usable format.

## Who Is Affected / Actors

Customers viewing their order history.

## Current Behaviour

Order history is only viewable on-screen, one page at a time.

## Use Cases

1. Customer clicks Export and downloads a CSV of their order history.

## Desired Outcomes

Customers can download their order history as a CSV file.

## Scope Boundaries

**In-Scope**: exporting the currently visible order history to CSV.
**Out-of-Scope**: exporting any other account data.
**Adjacent Concerns**: PDF export — not requested.

## Success Criteria

A downloaded CSV opens correctly in a spreadsheet application and matches the on-screen order history.

## Assumptions & Known Unknowns

**Assumed**: nothing beyond what's stated above.
**Unknown**: nothing material identified.
